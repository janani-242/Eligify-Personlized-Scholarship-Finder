WHAT IS ELIGIFY

Scholarship discovery, made simpler.

Eligify is a smart scholarship finder designed to help students discover opportunities that match their individual eligibility.

It brings relevant scholarship information together in one simple place, making the search easier, clearer and more personalized.

OUR PURPOSE

Making opportunities easier to find.

Finding the right scholarship can take time when students have to search through different sources and check requirements individually.

Eligify turns that search into a clear and personalized experience.
HOW IT WORKS
